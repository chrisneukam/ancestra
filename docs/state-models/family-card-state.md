# Family Card State

## Purpose

Defines the state required by a Family Card.

Family = Context

Family Cards are primary research workspaces.

---

# Family Card State

FamilyCard

- FamilyId
- Partners
- Children
- Timeline
- ResearchAssets

---

# Partners

One or more partner persons.

---

# Children

Connected child persons.

Children support graph traversal.

---

# Timeline

Family history visualization.

Examples:

- Marriage
- Births
- Deaths
- Moves

Timeline is a view.

Timeline is not the editor.

---

# Research Assets

Asset Types

- Sources
- Media
- Notes

Research Assets provide evidence for family research.

---

# Commands

Examples:

- AddPartner
- AddChild
- AddParent
- AddResearchEvidence
- OpenPerson

Commands modify family state.
