# UX Decision 005

# Research Assets Represent Evidence

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Sources, Media and Notes support genealogy research.

They do not represent identities or genealogy relationships.

---

## Decision

Research Assets = Evidence

Research Assets support research conclusions.

Asset Types:

- Sources
- Media
- Notes

---

## Consequences

Research Assets remain supporting objects.

Evidence can be attached to persons or families.

Evidence remains close to its research context.
