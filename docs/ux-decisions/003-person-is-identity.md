# UX Decision 003

# Person Represents Identity

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The genealogy graph contains persons, families, events and evidence.

The role of a Person must remain clear.

---

## Decision

Person = Identity

A Person represents an individual human identity.

Persons own genealogy facts.

Examples:

- Names
- Birth Information
- Death Information
- Occupations

---

## Consequences

Data belongs to persons.

Person Cards remain the primary editing surface for personal information.

Future features should treat Persons as identity objects.
