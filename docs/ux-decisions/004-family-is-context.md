# UX Decision 004

# Family Represents Context

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Researchers often investigate people through relationships and household structures.

A purely person-centered workflow does not sufficiently support genealogy research.

---

## Decision

Family = Context

Families provide the context in which genealogy research is performed.

Family Cards act as primary research workspaces.

---

## Consequences

Research happens in families.

Family Cards become central workspace objects.

Families support both navigation and research workflows.
