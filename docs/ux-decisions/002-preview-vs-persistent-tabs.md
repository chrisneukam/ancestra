# UX Decision 002

# Preview Tabs And Persistent Tabs

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Genealogy research is highly exploratory.

Users frequently navigate through persons and families before deciding where they want to work.

Traditional tab systems often create excessive tab clutter.

---

## Decision

Navigation opens Preview Tabs.

Editing creates Persistent Tabs.

Preview Tabs support exploration.

Persistent Tabs protect committed work.

---

## Consequences

Exploration remains lightweight.

Editing remains safe.

The workspace remains manageable during long research sessions.
