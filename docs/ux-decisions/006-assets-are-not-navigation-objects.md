# UX Decision 006

# Assets Are Not Navigation Objects

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The Navigator exists to support genealogy graph traversal.

Research Assets do not represent genealogy relationships.

---

## Decision

Research Assets are not primary navigation objects.

The Navigator primarily exposes:

- Persons
- Families
- Relationships

Research Assets remain accessible through:

- Person Cards
- Family Cards
- Search
- Inspector

---

## Consequences

The Navigator remains focused on genealogy.

Researchers navigate the graph rather than the database.
