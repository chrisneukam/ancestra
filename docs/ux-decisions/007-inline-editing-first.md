# UX Decision 007

# Inline Editing First

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Traditional genealogy applications frequently rely on dialogs, wizards and separate edit windows.

These patterns interrupt research flow.

---

## Decision

Inline Editing First.

Frequently edited information should be edited directly within its context.

Examples:

- Children
- Events
- Research Assets
- Notes

---

## Consequences

Context switching is reduced.

Research becomes faster and more fluid.

The application remains focused on genealogy rather than administration.
