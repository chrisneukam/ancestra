# Add Research Evidence

## Purpose

Defines the interaction flow for recording research evidence.

---

# Flow

Person Card
or
Family Card

↓

Research Assets Section

↓

User enters Asset Information

↓

Asset created

↓

Asset appears immediately

↓

New Empty Field appears

---

# Asset Types

- Source
- Media
- Note

---

# Focus

Workspace Focus remains unchanged.

Navigator remains unchanged.

Inspector refreshes context if required.

---

# Invariant

Research evidence is recorded inline.

The user remains within the current research context.
