# Add Research Evidence

## Purpose

This workflow records research evidence within an existing genealogy context.

Research evidence supports genealogy facts and research decisions.

Evidence may be recorded for:

- Persons
- Families

The workflow supports all research asset types:

- Sources
- Media
- Notes

The goal is to capture evidence without interrupting the research process.

---

# Entry Points

The workflow may begin from:

- Person Card
- Family Card

The active card provides the research context.

Evidence is always recorded within the current context.

---

# Workflow

Open Person Card
or
Open Family Card

↓

Navigate to Research Assets

↓

Enter Information Into An Empty Asset Field

↓

Confirm Input

↓

Research Asset Is Created

↓

New Empty Asset Field Appears

↓

Continue Research

# Inline Editing First

Research evidence should be entered directly within the active research context.

Users should not be required to initiate separate creation workflows before entering information.

The interface should remain ready for input at all times.

---

# Always Ready For Input

Research Asset sections should always provide an empty input field.

After evidence is recorded, a new empty field should immediately appear.

Researchers should be able to enter multiple records without additional interaction.

Example:

Sources

Church Record

Marriage Record

[ ]

The researcher never needs to explicitly request another input field.

---

# Minimize Context Switching

Recording evidence should not interrupt ongoing research.

Researchers should remain within the active Person Card or Family Card throughout the workflow.

The workflow should avoid:

- Modal dialogs
- Creation wizards
- Separate management views
- Unnecessary navigation

---

# Context First

Research evidence exists to support a research context.

Evidence should therefore be recorded where it is used.

Examples:

Birth Certificate
→ Person

Marriage Certificate
→ Family

Research Note
→ Current Research Context

Ancestra does not require researchers to organize evidence according to a rigid structure.

Evidence should be recorded where researchers naturally expect it.

---

# Consistent Behavior

The workflow should behave identically across all research contexts.

Person Card

↓

Research Assets

↓

Enter Evidence

Family Card

↓

Research Assets

↓

Enter Evidence

Researchers should learn one workflow that works everywhere.

---

# Design Goal

Recording evidence should feel like a natural continuation of research.

Researchers should focus on:

- discovering information
- evaluating evidence
- documenting findings

The software should stay out of the way.
